APPROACH
Design is solving problems,not decorating screens.
Research before pixels

Every screen starts as a question. I spend time with users, support tickets and analytics before Figma opens, so decisions trace back to evidence, not opinion.

Systems over screens

EA single beautiful screen doesn't scale. I design reusable components and patterns so every new feature ships faster and stays consistent.

Business meets usability

Good UX that misses the business case doesn't ship. I frame every recommendation in terms of conversion, retention, and support cost — not taste.

Accessibility by default

WCAG isn't a final checklist — it's a constraint from the first wireframe. Contrast, focus states and semantics are part of the design, not a patch after launch.

PROCESS
Case studies built for scale, trust and clarity.

01
Discover
Understand the business goal, constraints and who the work is really for before proposing any solution.
02
User Research
Talk to users, review data and audit the current experience to find where the real friction lives.
03
User journey
Map the end-to-end experience to see the problem in context, not as an isolated screen.
04
Wireframes
Structure the flow and layout at low fidelity, testing the logic before investing in visual design.
05
Prototype
Build an interactive version realistic enough to test real decisions and edge cases.
06
Testing
Validate with real users, and revise based on where they hesitate, misread, or get stuck.
07
Developer handoff
Document specs, states and components clearly so engineering can build with confidence.
08
Design QA
Ensures the final product matches the approved design and meets quality standards.
09
Launch
Ship, monitor real usage, and feed what's learned back into the next iteration.
